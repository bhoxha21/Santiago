Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you cast off.

Do I need a boat license?

No license is required! We provide a complete safety and operational orientation before you leave the dock.

Is there a wheelchair accessible entrance?

No, unfortunately our current boarding locations are not wheelchair accessible and there is no wheelchair accessible entrance.

What comes with the boat rental?

We provide a cooler with ice, premium Bluetooth speakers, all necessary fuel, and complete professional safety gear.

Does the tour include drinks?

While our tours provide coolers, guests are encouraged to bring their own favorite beverages and snacks to enjoy on the water.
